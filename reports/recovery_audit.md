## Executive summary

The repository contains a **working local enhancement pipeline** (`cloud_pipeline_runner.py`, `reconstruction_pipeline.py`) that produces playable `output/<map>/world.glb` assets with procedural interiors, vegetation, PBR textures, and physics metadata. The **new untracked** `cloud_reconstruction_factory.py` and `observation_and_decision_engine.py` are **misnamed**: they perform **local procedural synthesis**, not Hugging Face cloud inference. `configs/cloud_models.lock.json` lists cloud models as `ACTIVE_VERIFIED` **without evidence** of runtime verification in this repo.

`output/worlds/<map>/` (spec section 63) **does not exist**. `work/state.json` **does not exist**. `cache/cloud_ai/` is empty (no cloud cache hits on disk).

`reports/final_acceptance.json` and `src/build_acceptance_matrix.py` mark broad **PASS** statuses from **file existence checks**, not gameplay regression or live cloud job logs.
